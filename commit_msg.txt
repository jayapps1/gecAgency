feat: Production-grade Android Staff Operations App for Ghana Emergency Connect Agency

Comprehensive Staff Operations & Emergency Response Android Application for Ghana Emergency Connect Agency.

Key Capabilities & Architectural Highlights:
1. Glassmorphic Design System & Branding:
   - Centralized Material 3 color system (Deep Navy #0B1F33, Emergency Blue #1769AA, #F4F7FA surface).
   - Reusable GecGlassCard with translucent surfaces, soft borders, and elevation.
   - Official GEC Shield Emblem artwork (ic_gec_logo.xml) and white adaptive app launcher icon.
   - Translucent floating bottom navigation bar (GecBottomNavigation) with 24dp rounded corners.

2. Authentication & Security Hardening:
   - Password and TOTP 2-Step Authenticator login flows.
   - Segmented button selector, input validation, leading field icons, and inline error containers.
   - EncryptedSharedPreferences session storage with account-partitioned cache wiping on logout.
   - Zero password/TOTP persistence or logging.

3. Incident Operations Workspace:
   - Incident queue triage with live category search, filter chips (All Active, Received, Acknowledged, In Progress, Resolved).
   - Operational incident cards with GecStatusChip and explicit text SLA badges (On Time, Attention, Delayed with red border accent).
   - Incident Detail workspace with Hero Glass Card, narrative description, location card with Google Maps intent handoff, and append-only timeline history.
   - Operational Action Panel allowing authorized roles to acknowledge, start response actions, and resolve assignments.

4. Individual Responder Assignment & Dispatch:
   - Agency-scoped responder dispatch per ReportAssignment (multi-agency isolation).
   - Modal bottom sheet responder picker with active staff filtering and duplicate assignment prevention.
   - Release responder confirmation dialogs while preserving operational history.
   - Responder workspace ("My Active Incident Assignments") with read-only incident detail and location maps handoff.
   - Persistent StaffNotifications, FCM push signals, and SSE realtime events.

5. Staff Alert Center & Fleet Readiness:
   - Notifications screen with glass unread alert summary, category icons (Warning, Safety, Operational, System), and deep link navigation.
   - Appliance Fleet Readiness workspace with 2-column metric grid (Available, Dispatched, Maintenance, Out of Commission), search, and temporary station relocation badges.

6. Account Scope, Safety & Reviews:
   - ProfileScreen displaying initials avatar and OperationalScopeCard (Agency, Region, Station, Role).
   - SettingsScreen with GEC About card, security policy, development environment indicator, and Sign Out confirmation dialog.
   - SafetyInformationScreen with severity chips (INFO, ADVISORY, WARNING, CRITICAL) and advisory creation form.
   - ReviewListScreen with accessible 1-5 star rating displays and station-scoped feedback.
   - Role-customized dashboards for Agency Admin, Regional Admin, Station Admin, Control Room Operator, Responder (Read-Only), System Admin, and Restricted roles.
